# Market Intel Manifesto

> **Build tools that deserve trust.**

---

## Our Mission

Market Intel exists to help people make better trading decisions through evidence, transparency, and disciplined analysis.

We are not building an AI that claims to know the future.

We are building an AI that helps users better understand uncertainty.

---

# Our Principles

## 1. Evidence Over Opinion

Every conclusion must be supported by measurable evidence.

If we cannot explain why something was recommended, it should not be recommended.

---

## 2. Transparency Over Mystery

Users should always understand:

- Why a stock received its score.
- Why it was rejected.
- What assumptions were made.
- What data was used.
- What information may be missing.

The reasoning should never be hidden behind a black box.

---

## 3. Confidence Is Not Certainty

A high confidence score does not mean the AI is correct.

It means the available evidence is unusually strong.

The platform must always acknowledge uncertainty.

---

## 4. Every Trade Is an Experiment

Every signal becomes data.

Winning trades teach us.

Losing trades teach us.

Ignored trades teach us.

Nothing is wasted.

---

## 5. Never Hide Failure

Poor predictions should never disappear.

Every recommendation remains part of the historical record.

The system earns trust by admitting mistakes and learning from them.

---

## 6. Quality Over Quantity

Ten mediocre signals are worse than one excellent opportunity.

The platform should be willing to report:

> "No opportunities meet today's standard."

Silence is preferable to noise.

---

## 7. Humans Make the Final Decision

Market Intel is an analyst.

Not a portfolio manager.

Not a financial advisor.

Not an autonomous trader.

The final decision always belongs to the user.

---

## 8. Explain Every Score

Every score must be decomposable.

Users should see:

- Positive contributors
- Negative contributors
- Risk factors
- Missing information
- Confidence level

Nothing should be accepted on faith.

---

## 9. Risk Is a Feature

Most trading software highlights opportunity.

Market Intel gives equal attention to risk.

Every opportunity should clearly explain:

- What could go wrong
- What would invalidate the setup
- What evidence argues against the trade

The bear case is just as important as the bull case.

---

## 10. Simplicity Wins

Complexity should only be introduced when it produces measurable improvements.

Every additional model, feature, indicator, or dependency must earn its place.

---

## 11. Backtest Before Belief

Ideas are cheap.

Evidence is valuable.

No strategy should be trusted until it has demonstrated measurable performance over a meaningful sample size.

---

## 12. The AI Must Be Auditable

Every recommendation should be reproducible.

If the same inputs are provided, the reasoning should be understandable and reviewable.

The platform should leave an audit trail of its decisions.

---

## 13. Measure Everything

If something cannot be measured, it cannot be improved.

Track:

- Win rate
- Profit factor
- Drawdown
- Signal quality
- False positives
- False negatives
- Performance by market condition
- Performance by catalyst
- Performance by strategy

The platform should improve through measurement, not intuition.

---

## 14. Avoid Hype

Market Intel will never promise:

- Guaranteed profits
- Risk-free trading
- Secret strategies
- Easy money
- Certain winners

The market is uncertain.

Our language should reflect that reality.

---

## 15. Build for the Long Term

Shortcuts create technical debt.

Technical debt creates fragile software.

Fragile software destroys trust.

Every design decision should favor maintainability over temporary convenience.

---

## Engineering Principles

- Prefer readable code over clever code.
- Prefer explicit behavior over hidden magic.
- Keep functions focused.
- Write tests for critical logic.
- Document complex algorithms.
- Version important models and scoring systems.
- Keep business logic independent from user interfaces.
- Make every important decision traceable.

---

## Product Principles

The platform should:

- Explain before recommending.
- Warn before encouraging.
- Rank before predicting.
- Measure before optimizing.
- Learn before expanding.

---

## Success

Market Intel succeeds when users become more disciplined, more informed, and more aware of risk.

Not when they blindly follow signals.

---

## Our Promise

We will never confuse confidence with certainty.

We will never value hype over evidence.

We will never hide uncertainty.

Every recommendation should earn trust through transparency.

That is the standard every feature, every model, and every line of code must meet.
